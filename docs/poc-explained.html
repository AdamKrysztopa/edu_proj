The idea in one sentence

Make expert reasoning
teachable.

A focused physics learning workflow that captures what an expert leaves implicit, identifies a learner’s gap, and tests whether teaching the missing operation helps the learner solve a new problem.

Proposed routeFirst-year mechanicsHuman-reviewed AIEffectiveness unproven
What it will be

One complete learning case

A researcher-led workflow for selecting and combining conservation principles in mechanics.

What it needs

People + evidence + tools

Physics experts, learner responses, reviewed tasks, a reliable capture tool and a small instructional module.

What we expect

A defensible next decision

A working process, traceable outputs and evidence to continue, revise or stop the selected approach.

Planning first. We are choosing the route before resuming implementation. This brief makes the candidate PoC concrete; it does not claim that the 13-method comparison is complete or that learning benefits have been demonstrated.

02 / The complete workflowEvidence before a teaching decision

Six steps. One traceable chain.

Every instructional choice should lead back to a learner response and a reviewed operation. AI can propose a question or interpretation; people establish whether the evidence supports it.

The expert

Solves naturally first, then explains cues and choices. Retrospective probing must not contaminate the original task performance.

The researcher

Reviews traces and codes, checks alternative explanations, chooses materials and controls the comparison.

The learner

Explains an attempt, practises a targeted operation and solves a new task independently.

What AI contributes: structured retrospective questions and candidate interpretations. In the first learning comparison, fixed, human-reviewed instruction keeps AI delivery effects separate from the value of the elicited operation.

03 / A worked caseIllustrative · not participant data

The hidden step may be
where the problem changes.

A projectile embeds in a suspended block. The combined mass rises. The useful operation is to split the event into stages and check which conservation principle applies to each.

What we observeWhat we should checkWhat the PoC does
Learner conserves mechanical energy across the whole event.Do they understand inelastic collisions, or have they failed to separate the stages?Ask where energy changes form, then ask them to mark the stage boundary.
Learner chooses valid principles but makes an algebra error.Is the difficulty procedural rather than a missing expert operation?Give mathematical support instead of teaching a new principle-selection strategy.
Learner succeeds after seeing the example.Can they explain and apply the stage distinction in another context?Use a new, independently attempted problem and a later transfer check.
Targeted prompt

“Where does the governing principle change, and why?”

The learner commits an answer before seeing the model explanation, then applies the same operation to a new case.

Operation record

Partition → select → justify

Store the cue, stage boundary, principle conditions, supporting trace, known failure cases and linked diagnostic question.

This example shows what an operation might look like. Whether experts omit it, learners need it and teaching it improves transfer are separate empirical questions.

06 / Expected resultsOutputs are commitments · learning gains are hypotheses

What would we have
at the end?

A documented route, an operable PoC and an evidence package. We hope the recovered operations help learners transfer their knowledge; the evaluation must also be able to show that they do not.

Continue

Evidence supports the intended next claim. Preserve the effective components and plan the next validation step.

Revise

Diagnoses are ambiguous, delivery fails or uncertainty is too large. Change the relevant component and retest.

Stop or narrow

Operations lack support, the bottleneck lies elsewhere, or a sound comparison rules out worthwhile benefit.

Set thresholds before collecting outcome data. The separate Stage A/B draft already contains topic-specific gates: K0’s 30% error-category screen, K1’s operation-yield and contradiction criteria, and K2’s futility rule at d = 0.30 with a compliance condition. Adopt the complete protocol if that study is selected; these numbers are not predicted PoC results. The draft’s delayed assessment is 2–3 weeks after instruction.
