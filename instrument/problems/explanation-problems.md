# Ordinary-explanation problems (Stage A session step 1, paper)

**Status:** draft for the physicist check, together with A1–B2 in `problems.json`. These two problems go on the paper handout for the ordinary explanation ("Write a worked explanation of these two problems for a first-year student"). They are kept out of `problems.json` because the app never shows them, and because the guard treats words from `problems.json` statements as non-leading. Adding these statements there would widen that set.

- E_A has set A's structure: a perfectly inelastic collision, then gravity.
- E_B has set B's structure: a collision, then a spring.
- Their surfaces differ from A1–B2, from the K0 items (`instrument/k0/items.md`) and from the roadmap's illustrative items.

Use g = 9.8 m/s². Friction and air resistance are negligible.

**E_A.** A 0.20 kg ball moving horizontally at 5.0 m/s strikes and sticks to a 0.80 kg bob hanging at rest from a light string 1.0 m long. What is the largest angle the string makes with the vertical?

**E_B.** A 3.0 kg cart moving at 2.0 m/s on a level frictionless track collides head-on and elastically with a 1.0 kg cart at rest. The 1.0 kg cart then runs straight into an ideal spring (k = 300 N/m) at the end of the track, initially uncompressed, with its far end fixed. What is the maximum compression of the spring?

## Answer key

| Problem | Stage 1 | Stage 2 | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| E_A | Momentum: v = (0.20 × 5.0) / 1.0 = 1.0 m/s | h = v² / 2g = 0.051 m; cos θ = 1 − h / L = 0.949 | θ ≈ 18° |
| E_B | Elastic: v₂ = 2m₁ v₁ / (m₁ + m₂) = 2 × 3.0 × 2.0 / 4.0 = 3.0 m/s | x = v √(m/k) = 3.0 × √(1.0/300) | 0.17 m |
