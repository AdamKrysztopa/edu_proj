# Consent to take part in a pilot interview

**Study:** How expert physicists select and combine conservation principles, and whether an AI interviewer can help make that reasoning visible.
**Researcher:** [name, institution, email]

## What you will do

About 75 minutes, in one sitting:
1. Write a short worked explanation of two mechanics problems for a first-year student (on paper).
2. Solve four problems while thinking aloud and drawing on a tablet.
3. Answer questions about your solutions. One set of questions comes from an AI interviewer shown on screen, the other from a human interviewer.
4. A five-minute conversation about how the questions felt.

This is a **pilot**. Its purpose is to test the procedure and build the coding scheme; it is not the main study's data.

## What is recorded

Your voice, your drawings and written work, the questions you were asked, and your answers. You are identified only by a code (for example P01). The list linking the code to your name is kept separately from the recordings.

## Where your data go

To run the session, some data are sent to these services over encrypted connections:
- **ElevenLabs** and **OpenAI**: your voice recordings, to turn speech into text. Both are used, to compare their accuracy.
- **Anthropic**: the text of what you said and images of your drawings, to generate and check the AI interviewer's questions.

Each service processes the data under its API data-processing terms. [Add, for each: whether data are used for training and how long they are retained, as confirmed before the session.]

Everything else stays on the researcher's laptop, which is encrypted, and is not shared.

## How your data are used

- To develop the coding scheme and improve the procedure.
- Short excerpts may be quoted in publications with anything identifying removed. Your name will never appear.
- Recordings are deleted by [date or event, e.g. the end of the study].

## Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time, skip any question, or withdraw afterwards until [date], without giving a reason; your recordings and transcripts are then deleted.
