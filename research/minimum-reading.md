# Minimum reading

**This page is the entire minimum.** Every book, paper, branch note and detailed protocol is an optional extra. Nothing else needs to be read before continuing.

## The question

How can AI discover what experts leave implicit, diagnose what a learner is missing, and turn that gap into effective instruction? Physics is the first setting; the question extends beyond physics.

## The working idea

Make hidden expert operations explicit → diagnose the learner's bottleneck → choose modelling, practice or feedback → check learning and transfer → adapt.

This is a hypothesis to test. A difficulty might instead come from missing prerequisites, a misconception or insufficient practice. An expert's explanation or a fluent AI reconstruction does not by itself validate an operation.

## What the research suggests

The [research map](../ai_education_research_map.md) records the evidence and its limits:

- Decoding the Disciplines frames the bottleneck; Cognitive Task Analysis supplies candidate elicitation methods. Compare ordinary explanations, observed problem solving and retrospective probes.
- Learner diagnosis is a separate task. Expert interviews alone cannot tell us what a particular student needs.
- Worked examples, self-explanation, practice and feedback are candidate instructional responses. Test whether the recovered operations improve learning.
- Measure unassisted performance, delayed retention and transfer. Build adaptation only after finding useful signals.

These are research directions, not findings from our own participants. The literature reviews are complete; the project's premise remains empirically unvalidated.

## The route and the next step

The route is chosen ([roadmap](roadmap.md)):

1. Coded student errors show where learners get stuck.
2. Experts solve problems aloud and are then asked about their recorded work. Only steps the recording confirms, shared by at least 3 of 12 experts, count.
3. Those steps are taught through a fixed module of worked examples, with prompts asking students to explain each step and feedback written in advance for each type of error.
4. Learning is judged on new problems, two to three weeks later, without help.

AI works behind the scenes: it helps interview experts (under test), drafts materials and assists coding. It does not tutor students directly in the first version. Personalisation waits until the simple version shows what is worth adapting.

The route is justified by the evidence but not yet proven. Ten assumptions are listed, each with the milestone that tests it and what changes if it fails.

**Next:** two things in parallel. First, a short quiz in a first-year course checks whether choosing and combining principles really is where students fail; if not, the route changes before any expert is recruited. Second, the expert-interview tool is finished and piloted with one or two physicists. No additional reading is required from you.
