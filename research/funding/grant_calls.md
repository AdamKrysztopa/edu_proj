# Grant and funding calls (as of 2026-10-25)

**Summary.** The three best fits are these. **Erasmus+ KA220-HED** fits best: an education tool piloted with a PW lecturer and students is what cooperation partnerships fund, and PW can apply. The 2027 deadline is expected in early March 2027 (unverified). Second is **NCN OPUS**: the residual-prediction question (RQ-B) is basic research, and the PI needs only one publication, not a PhD. OPUS 32 closes 15 Dec 2026; OPUS 33 is expected around June 2027 (unverified). Third is **Horizon Europe CL4-2027-02-DIGITAL-EMERGING-52**, which asks in its own text for AI that "capture[s] expert knowledge" and cuts the time new employees need to get up to speed. Its stage 1 closes 2 Feb 2027, but it needs a manufacturing consortium. If a company is founded, the strongest route for it is the **EIC Pre-Accelerator – Widening** (deadline 18 Nov 2027).

Any deadline or amount not confirmed on an official page is marked "(unverified)".

## Notes per call

All URLs were accessed on 2026-10-25.

1. **Erasmus+ KA220-HED.** https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/programme-guide/part-b/key-action-2/cooperation-partnerships (acc. 2026-10-25). The figures (5 March deadline, the three lump sums, ≥3 organisations from 3 countries, applicant ≥2 years old) come from the current guide. The 2027 guide is not yet published. It is the cleanest fit for professors → students: PW coordinates and runs the POC, and partners replicate it.
2. **NCN OPUS.** https://www.ncn.gov.pl/ogloszenia/konkursy/opus32 and https://www.ncn.gov.pl/finansowanie-nauki/konkursy/harmonogram (acc. 2026-10-25). OPUS 32 allows plain OPUS without foreign partners. Eligible applicants include "osoba fizyczna" (an individual). A person may be PI on only one OPUS/SONATA proposal per edition. With seven weeks left, OPUS 33 (spring 2027, following NCN's usual March–June pattern) is the realistic target, once the D1/N3 results are in hand.
3. **CL4 DIGITAL-EMERGING-52.** https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/horizon-cl4-2027-02-digital-emerging-52-two-stage (acc. 2026-10-25). Proposals must cover ≥2 fields, one of which can be "Human-AI Co-Learning and knowledge capture". The setting is advanced manufacturing, so the project would be a technology partner here, not the coordinator.
4. **EIC Pre-Accelerator – Widening.** https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-pre-accelerator_en (acc. 2026-10-25). Single SMEs or small mid-caps only, from widening countries. The best route for the organisational knowledge-at-risk wedge once a company exists.
5. **FNP PoC FENG.** https://www.fnp.org.pl/en/component/fnp_programs/program/proof-of-concept-poc-feng-en-1 and https://www.fnp.org.pl/component/fnp_programs/program/proof-of-concept-feng/nabor-1-2026-1 (acc. 2026-10-25). It validates existing research results, so it suits the step after the WUT POC. The lead is employed by the applicant at ≥0.5 FTE.
6. **NCN SONATA 22.** https://www.ncn.gov.pl/ogloszenia/konkursy/sonata22 (acc. 2026-10-25). Basic research, any of the 26 panels.
7. **NCN PRELUDIUM 26.** Inferred from the harmonogram link in note 2: PRELUDIUM 25 ran 16 Mar–16 Jun 2026, and the 2027 schedule is not yet published.
8. **NCBR LIDER UP.** https://www.gov.pl/web/ncbr/lider-up---i-konkurs (acc. 2026-10-25). Three phases (training, project, pre-deployment). An individual submits, and a research organisation employs the team.
9. **POLNORIS.** https://www.gov.pl/web/ncbr/polnoris--laczymy-kraje-wiedze-i-innowacje (acc. 2026-10-25). Open topics; industrial research and experimental development.
10. **FNP FIRST TEAM 2/2026.** https://www.fnp.org.pl/en/component/fnp_programs/program/first-team-feng/call-2-2026en-GB (acc. 2026-10-25). Applications are accepted until 20.11.2026. Amount ("4 mln zł") and PI eligibility ("od 2 do 9 lat po doktoracie"): programme page https://www.fnp.org.pl/component/fnp_programs/program/first-team-feng (acc. 2026-10-25).
11. **EIC Pathfinder.** https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-pathfinder_en (acc. 2026-10-25). The EIC holds an online info day on 2027 opportunities on 19 Nov 2026.
12. **EIC Accelerator.** https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en (acc. 2026-10-25).
13. **EIC Transition.** https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-transition_en (acc. 2026-10-25). Requires results from eligible EU research projects (EIC Pathfinder, ERC PoC, Horizon Europe Pillar II RIAs, Horizon 2020 Societal Challenges and LEIT RIAs, Research Infrastructures RIAs, civil or dual-use European Defence Fund research), at TRL 3 completed or TRL 4.
14. **CL2 TRANSFO-05.** https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/horizon-cl2-2027-01-transfo-05 (acc. 2026-10-25). The age group (pre-primary and primary) and the policy-evidence aim sit far from the product.
15. **CL2 TRANSFO-06.** https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/horizon-cl2-2027-01-transfo-06 (acc. 2026-10-25).
16. **PARP Granty na Eurogranty.** https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/content/article/89045:polskie-innowacje-maja-szanse-podbic-europe-rusza-nowy-nabor (acc. 2026-10-25). PARP's site blocked automated access, so nothing here is confirmed.
17. **Ścieżka SMART.** https://www.gov.pl/web/ncbr/przestrzen-dla-odwaznych-technologii-ncbr-z-szeroka-oferta-konkursowa-dla-nauki-i-biznesu-w-2026-roku (acc. 2026-10-25).
